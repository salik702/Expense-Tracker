Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, log expenses, or use our service. This may include:

  • Personal information (name, email address)
  • Expense data (amount, category, date, description)
  • Usage data (how you interact with our service)
  • Device and technical information

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide, maintain, and improve our service. Specifically, we may use your information to:

  • Provide and personalize your expense tracking experience
  • Send you important updates and service notifications
  • Improve our service based on user feedback and usage patterns
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our platform
  • Communicate with you about your account and our service

3. Data Storage

We store your data securely using industry-standard encryption and security practices. Your expense data is stored privately and is accessible only to you unless you choose to share it. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our service.

4. Third Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to help operate and improve our service. These service providers may have access to your information only as needed to perform their functions and are obligated to maintain the confidentiality of your data. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable
  • Export your data in a portable format

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of our service after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.
